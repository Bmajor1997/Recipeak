Relish Privacy Policy

Effective November 27, 2026

Relish is an app that suggests a recipe from a photo of a meal or a drink. It is made by Benjamin Major ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information Relish uses, why, and the choices you have. Relish was previously called Recipeak; only the name has changed. If you have a question, contact me at turelapps@gmail.com.

The short version

  • I don't sell your information, and Relish shows no ads.
  • Relish has no analytics or tracking tools.
  • Photos you send are used to find a recipe. They aren't kept on my server or added to your Saved recipes.
  • Your saved recipes stay on your phone.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time.

Information Relish uses

Your account

To use Relish you sign in with an email address and password, with your Google account, or (on iPhone) with Sign in with Apple. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication, which stores your email address and, if you use one, a protected form of your password. I never see your password. With Google or Apple, Relish receives only your name and email address from them, never your Google or Apple password.

Photos you send

When you take or choose a photo and tap Send, the photo goes to the Relish server and then to OpenAI, which identifies the dish or drink. If Relish already has that recipe, it opens on your phone. If not, Relish asks you first, and only if you agree does OpenAI write a recipe (and make a generic photo of the dish) from the dish's name. Recipes made this way are kept on the server, without your photo or anything that identifies you, so the next person who photographs the same dish gets them straight away. The server asks OpenAI not to store the request. The Relish server doesn't save your photo. Your photo isn't used as a recipe's cover picture; cover pictures come from a shared library of generic dish photos.

Don't send photos of people or anything private. Relish only needs the food or drink.

Usage records

To enforce free limits and keep costs under control, the server keeps a record of each request: which account made it, when, what kind of request it was, and how much it cost. These records don't contain your photos or recipes.

Feedback and ratings

If you send a feature request or bug report from Settings, or report a recipe from its page, the server keeps what you wrote (and, for a recipe report, the recipe's name and your reason), linked to your account, for up to one year. If you rate a recipe or note that you made it, the rating and any note are kept for up to two years so I can improve recipes.

Subscriptions

If you subscribe, payment is handled by Google Play (or the App Store on iPhone). I never see your card details. The server checks your subscription status with RevenueCat, a subscription service, using your account's ID.

On your phone

Saved recipes, their cover photos, your ratings, recently viewed dishes, and your settings are stored on your phone, separately for each account. Signing out keeps them on the phone for when you sign in again. Deleting your account removes them.

Camera and photo access

Relish asks for camera access to take photos and for photo library access when you choose a photo. It doesn't use your microphone or location.

Services Relish relies on

  • Google Firebase: sign-in (email, Google and Apple).
  • Google and Apple: Continue with Google and Sign in with Apple, if you choose them.
  • OpenAI: identifying dishes and writing recipes from photos.
  • RevenueCat, Google Play and the App Store: subscriptions.
  • A cloud hosting provider that runs the Relish server.

These services handle information only to provide their part of Relish, under their own privacy policies.

Deleting your account and data

In the app, go to Settings, then Account, then Delete account. After you confirm your password (or your Google or Apple account), Relish deletes your sign-in, your subscription record at RevenueCat, your feedback and ratings on the server, and your saved recipes and settings on that phone. Usage records are kept only as costs, with your account ID removed, because they are part of monthly totals. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription; cancel it in Google Play or the App Store.

If you can't use the app, contact me at turelapps@gmail.com and I'll delete your account for you.

How long information is kept

  • Account: until you delete it.
  • Feedback: up to one year.
  • Ratings and notes: up to two years.
  • Usage records: kept for cost and limit records; the account ID is removed when you delete your account.

Security

Information travels over encrypted connections. Every request to the server must come from a signed-in account, and the server's records are stored privately. No system is perfectly secure, but I work to protect your information.

Age

Relish is for people 18 and older. It includes recipes for alcoholic drinks, which are only for people of legal drinking age where they live. I don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has created an account, contact me and I'll delete it.

Your choices and rights

You can see and change your saved recipes and settings in the app, and delete your account at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your information, or to object to how it is used. To make a request, contact me at turelapps@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I'll update the date at the top. For significant changes, I'll let you know in the app.
